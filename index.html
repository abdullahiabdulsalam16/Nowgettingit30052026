About 3mmt

3mtt stands for "3 million technical talent" — an initiative where nigeria enrol to learn software development and other technical skills. The 3mmt class is designed to help participants present their research to the world.

Key points

  • Present complex ideas simply and engagingly.
  • Focus on clarity, structure and storytelling.
  • Participants must use a single static PowerPoint slide.

Judging criteria

  1. Comprehension
  2. Engagement
  3. Communication
